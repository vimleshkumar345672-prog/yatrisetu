YatriSetu Help Centre

How does Passenger Verification work?

Users can register once with their Name, Mobile Number, Govt ID last 4 digits, and Photo. Every booking attaches this proof so the vehicle owner knows exactly who is travelling.

How do I publish a ride or room with photos?

Click 'Offer a ride' at the top, select Ride or Homestay, fill in vehicle number or address details, and upload photos directly from your phone or computer.

How long do listings stay active?

To keep our feed fresh and accurate, all rides and bookings can be scheduled up to 2 weeks (14 days) in advance and are automatically cleaned up after 14 days.

Where can I see my confirmed bookings?

All your confirmed tickets are saved in your browser inside the 'My Bookings' section at the top bar—with direct WhatsApp and Phone Call buttons.
